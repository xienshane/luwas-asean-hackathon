# Privacy Notes

LUWAS handles sensitive volunteer and disaster-response data. The first implementation must:

- Minimize volunteer PII collection.
- Protect volunteer names, phone numbers, and precise GPS with Supabase row-level security.
- Allow coordinators to see operationally necessary data only.
- Define retention rules for precise GPS and contact data.
- Document alignment with the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012, Republic Act No. 10173.
